# 🏖️ Beachy-Ball

**Beachy-Ball** is a fast-paced, minimalist arcade game built with **Unity** for Android. Guide a rolling beach ball across an endless boardwalk while dodging oncoming obstacles. With smooth controls, colorful beach visuals, and endless challenge, it’s the perfect casual game for quick, addictive sessions.

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- 🏃 Your beach ball rolls forward automatically.
- 🎯 Swipe left or right to dodge incoming obstacles.
- 🔁 The game gets progressively faster and harder.
- 🌟 Survive as long as you can and beat your high score.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🎮 **Simple Controls** – Just swipe left or right to dodge.
- 🌊 **Beach-Inspired Visuals** – Relaxing yet vibrant design.
- 📈 **Endless Runner Mechanics** – Speed and difficulty scale over time.
- 🔊 **Sound Effects & Music** – Optional immersive audio.
- 📱 **Optimized for Android** – Smooth performance even on mid-range devices.

## Getting Started

### Open the Project in Unity Hub

1. Launch Unity Hub.  
2. Click **Open** and navigate to the **Beachy-Ball** project folder.  
3. Select the folder to open the project.

### Switch to Android Platform

1. Go to **File > Build Settings**.  
2. Select **Android** and click **Switch Platform**.  
3. Ensure you have **Android Build Support** installed.

### Connect Your Android Device

1. Enable **Developer Options** and **USB Debugging** on your Android device.  
2. Connect your device via USB.

### Build and Run

1. In Unity, click **File > Build Settings > Build and Run**.  
2. The game will compile and launch on your connected device.
